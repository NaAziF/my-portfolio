Work

End-to-end Voice AI calling platform

A complete outbound calling system: the agent dials leads, holds a natural conversation, answers from a custom knowledge base, and stores every call for review. Each agent handles 10 hours of calls a day, and 10 run at once.

  • Real-time conversation on LiveKit, with voice activity detection (VAD) for natural turn-taking, Deepgram for speech-to-text, Groq as the LLM, and Cartesia or Amazon Polly for voice.
  • Outbound calls placed through LiveKit SIP and Plivo, with Redis handling call state.
  • Every call's transcript is saved to MongoDB and its recording to Amazon S3.
  • RAG on Pinecone keeps answers grounded in the company's own content.
  • Runs on AWS EC2 with Docker, Nginx and Jenkins CI/CD.
